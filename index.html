About AutoAdvisor

Car research made easier.

AutoAdvisor is a static front-end car research tool focused on practical used-car decisions. It is built around searchable vehicle data, guided recommendations, clickable comparison cards, and AAS scoring.

What AAS means

AAS stands for AutoAdvisor Score. It summarizes reliability, value, MPG, ownership cost, safety, mileage life, and resale value into one quick score after a model year is selected.

How to use it

Use AAS as a starting point, then compare the car's price, mileage life, common issues, and real condition before buying.

Data note

Prices and ownership notes are estimates for research and demo purposes. Images should be real vehicle photos or approved image files, not fake drawings. Users should verify current market prices, history reports, inspections, and official specs before purchase.
